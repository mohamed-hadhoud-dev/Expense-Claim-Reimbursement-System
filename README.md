## Project Overview

The Expense Claim Reimbursement System is designed to make the process
of submitting, reviewing, approving, and tracking work-related expense
claims easier and more organized.

In the current process, employees may pay work-related expenses from
their own money and then submit claims with supporting receipts. Manual,
paper-based records can make claims difficult to organize, review, and
track, which may delay reimbursement.

The proposed system aims to provide a centralized way for employees to
submit expense claims and receipts, while allowing authorized reviewers
to review claims and approve or reject them.

## Main Goals

-   Reduce the time and effort required to submit and review expense
    claims.
-   Reduce errors caused by manual data entry and paper-based records.
-   Organize receipts and supporting documents so they are easier to
    retrieve.
-   Allow employees to track the status of their claims.
-   Maintain organized records of submitted, approved, rejected, and
    reimbursed claims.
-   Protect financial and personal information through authentication
    and role-based access.

## Planned / Analyzed Features

-   User authentication and role-based access.
-   Creating and submitting expense claims.
-   Recording claim details such as date, category, amount, and
    description.
-   Attaching receipts and supporting documents.
-   Reviewing, approving, or rejecting claims by authorized reviewers.
-   Tracking claim status.
-   Basic search and reporting.

## Project Documentation

The project work includes system identification, the system request,
feasibility analysis, development methodology, time estimation using
PERT, a Gantt chart, and fact-finding activities such as interview
questions and a questionnaire.
